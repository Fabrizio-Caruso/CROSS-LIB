DRAGONMASTER (BETA)
Cross-Lib version
by Fabrizio Caruso

Original game (for the unexpanded Commodore VIC-20) by David Berdan
Published in "Compute!" magazine in 1983

CROSS-LIB VERSION:
This version is meant for 200+ systems.
Its gameplay is similar to the original game but not identical.
Graphics assets have been ripped from the original game for the most part. 
The Cross-Lib version has 4 consecutive levels. It keeps track of points and records.
The original game let you choose one level and the game was only on a single level. No points and no records.

GAMEPLAY:
- The goal
Your knights must defeat 4 dragon kings to save the princess. 
The game starts with 3 knights.
In order to kill a dragon king no other dragon has to be present.
If there are other dragons left, the dragon king is invincible.

- The dragons 
The chest of the dragons is the only vulnerable part.
Dragons instantly kill you if you are directly in front of their mouth or above their heads.
Dragons do not move but can randomly flip from one side to the other.
Moreover new dragons are spawned randomly. So, you need to be fast.

- The wizard
As the game progresses an invincible wizard will chase you at an increasing speed.

- Scoring
25     points for a dragon
0-150  as time bonus
50-200 for the dragon king

- Extra lives
An extra life is awarded every 1000 points.
